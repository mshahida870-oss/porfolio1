The model. Motion is integrated with a fourth-order Runge–Kutta scheme using quadratic drag, a = −g ĵ − k|v|v, where k is the drag coefficient per unit mass in units of 1/m. Setting k = 0 recovers the analytic parabola exactly, which is why the two curves coincide at the left end of the air-resistance slider.
